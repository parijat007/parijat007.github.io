How the record works

Each time a brief is published or changed, a line is added to a public log: the file, its SHA-256 fingerprint, and the fingerprint of the line before it. Editing or deleting any earlier line breaks every line after it, and a brief changed without a new line no longer matches its fingerprint. The log holds {{ ledger.total }} entries for {{ ledger.briefs }} briefs.

Read it as ledger.jsonl, or see how to verify this site.
